Personal research tool

Daily market research, saved to your Drive.

Daily Market Review runs on a Windows PC, summarizes stock screener results and market information, and saves a compact report to the Google Drive account its user authorizes.

What the application does

The desktop workflow collects market prices, calculates stock screening indicators, and combines selected news and economic-calendar information into a daily report. Its coverage includes the S&P 500, a technology stock list, and the Magnificent Seven.

The report is research material. It does not execute trades or provide personalized investment advice.

Why Google Drive access is requested

After the user signs in and grants permission, the uploader creates a report file in their Drive and updates that same file on later runs. It reads the file's metadata to verify delivery.

The application requests the limited drive.file permission for files it creates or that the user explicitly makes available to it. The uploader uses that permission only for its report file.

Where your information stays

The report remains in the user's Google Drive with its existing sharing settings. This website provides application information only; it does not display reports or connect to Google accounts.

A user may separately connect their Drive to ChatGPT to review the report. That connection requires its own authorization.
